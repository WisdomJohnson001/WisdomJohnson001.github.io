Lectures at FUNAAB started on **September 21, 2026**.

I wasn't there.

Not because I didn't want to be. Not because I was working this time.

Because I don't have a place to stay.

## The Hostel Situation

When the hostel bidding window opened, I didn't bid.

Here's why: FUNAAB hostels are crowded. I'm talking about **6 people in a room** that's barely bigger than a cubicle. Bunk beds stacked, no personal space, no quiet.

I don't like that. I've never liked that.

So I thought: *"I'll find my own place. I have time."*

I didn't.

## What I Was Thinking

Honestly? I thought I had more time than I did.

The admission came September 12. Orientation was September 14–19. Lectures started September 21.

That's **9 days** from admission to first lecture.

I was still processing the admission. Still working. Still thinking I had a few weeks to sort out accommodation.

I didn't.

## The Reality Now

- Lectures have started
- I'm not on campus
- I'm not in a hostel
- I don't have a place of my own
- I'm behind before I've even begun

This thing is unfolding rapidly in my face, and I'm caught off guard.

## How I Feel

**Stressed.** That's the honest answer.

I made a decision not to bid for hostel because I wanted better living conditions. That decision was valid. But I didn't follow it up with a plan for an alternative.

I assumed time was on my side. It wasn't.

## What I'm Doing About It

I'm not writing this to complain. I'm writing it because I need to solve this — and fast.

Here's my plan:

- **Search for off-campus accommodation** — asking around, checking WhatsApp groups, talking to classmates
- **Ask my classmates** — some might know of available rooms or agents
- **Consider temporary options** — staying with a relative or friend near campus while I figure it out
- **Reach out to the department** — explain the situation, see if there's any support
- **Attend as soon as I have a place** — even if I'm behind, I need to start

## What I Learned (The Hard Way)

| Lesson | Takeaway |
|--------|----------|
| **Don't assume you have time** | University moves fast. Days, not weeks |
| **Have a plan B before you need it** | Saying "no" to hostel means "yes" to finding an alternative — immediately |
| **Ask for help early** | I should have asked classmates the moment I got admitted |
| **Being proactive isn't optional** | I was proactive about the WhatsApp group. I should have been proactive about housing too |

## This Is Still My Journey

I'm documenting the wins and the failures. This is a failure.

But it's not the end of the story. It's just a rough chapter.

Lectures started without me. That's a fact. But I'll get there.

## What's Next

- **Find accommodation** — this week, somehow
- **Catch up on lectures** — notes from classmates
- **Show up** — even if I'm late, I need to start
- **Learn from this** — plan ahead, always

The journey continues. Even when it doesn't go as planned.
