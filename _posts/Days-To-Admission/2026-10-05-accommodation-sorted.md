If you read my last post, you know the situation: lectures had started, and I had nowhere to stay.

That's changed now.

## The Arrangement

A friend of mine — also a FUNAAB student — has a single room at **Oluwo**. He agreed to host me. We'll share the room and split the cost.

I went to check it out.

## The Room

Honest review? It's small.

Not *tiny* small. But smaller than I expected.

I'm used to a large house. Space. Room to move. My own corner. This is not that.

It's one room. Two people. Our stuff. That's it.

## How I Feel About It

Here's the truth: **this isn't what I wanted.**

I turned down hostel because I didn't want 6 people in a cubicle. Now I'm sharing a single room with one other person, and it's smaller than I'd like.

Is it ironic? A little.

But here's the difference:

- **Hostel:** 6 people, no choice, no control
- **This:** 1 roommate, my choice, my arrangement, my friend

It's not perfect. But it's **mine** — and it's a solution.

## Why I'm Accepting It

I don't have the luxury of waiting for perfect.

Lectures have already started. I'm already behind. Every day I spend looking for a better room is another day of missed classes.

So I'm taking this. I'm managing.

**I've got no choice but to manage with him.**

And that's okay. This is temporary. This is a starting point. This is how the journey begins — not in comfort, but in motion.

## What This Teaches Me

| Lesson | Takeaway |
|--------|----------|
| **Perfect is the enemy of done** | Waiting for the ideal room meant having no room |
| **A friend with a room is a blessing** | He didn't have to share. He chose to |
| **Small is better than nothing** | I can study, sleep, and show up to lectures |
| **This is temporary** | It's not forever. It's for now |

## What's Next

- **Move in** — get my stuff there, settle in
- **Start lectures** — I'm behind, but I'm starting
- **Catch up** — notes, classmates, effort
- **Make it work** — small room, big goals

The accommodation chapter is closed. The academic chapter begins.
