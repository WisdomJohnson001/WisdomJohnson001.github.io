FUNAAB's orientation week was **September 14–19, 2026**.

I wasn't there.

I was working.

## The Situation

When the admission came through on September 12, everything moved fast. Registration, documentation, planning — and then the reality hit: **orientation week was starting in two days.**

But I had work commitments I couldn't walk away from. So while my new classmates were on campus, learning the ropes, meeting lecturers, and figuring out where everything was — I was at work.

No orientation for me.

## What I Missed

Here's what I know happened that week:

- **Campus tours** — learning where the engineering faculty is, where lectures hold
- **Departmental introductions** — meeting the lecturers and staff
- **Registration guidance** — help with the paperwork I'd have to figure out alone
- **Meeting classmates** — putting faces to the WhatsApp group names
- **Student union activities** — the social side of starting uni

I missed all of it.

## How I Feel About It

Honestly? A bit behind.

Everyone else already knows:
- Where the buildings are
- Who the key lecturers are
- How registration works
- Who their friends are

I'm starting from zero on all of that.

But here's the thing:

**I'm still admitted. I'm still a Mechatronics Engineering student at FUNAAB. And I'll catch up.**

## My Plan to Catch Up

- **Ask questions** — the WhatsApp group is still active, and my classmates are helpful
- **Arrive early** — get to campus before lectures start, walk around, learn the layout
- **Introduce myself** — to lecturers, staff, and classmates
- **Take notes** — document everything so I don't forget

Missing orientation isn't the end. It's just a different starting point.

## What I Learned

Sometimes life doesn't line up perfectly with the academic calendar.

You can't always be in the room when everyone else is. But you can:
- **Acknowledge it** — don't pretend it didn't happen
- **Adapt** — find another way to get the information
- **Move forward** — the degree doesn't wait, and neither should you

## What's Next

- **Registration** — figuring it out step by step
- **First Lectures** — showing up, even if I'm nervous
- **Meeting My Classmates** — in person this time
- **Catching Up** — one day at a time

The journey continues. Even the missed parts are part of the story.
