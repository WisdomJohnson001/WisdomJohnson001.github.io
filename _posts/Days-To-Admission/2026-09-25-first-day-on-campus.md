Friday, **September 25, 2026** — my first time stepping foot on FUNAAB campus as an admitted student.

I wasn't there for lectures. I wasn't there to move in. I was there for one specific reason: **to collect the urine bottle for my medical tests** scheduled for Monday.

But even a quick trip like that left an impression.

## The Journey

I traveled down from my house that Friday morning. It wasn't a long trip, but it was a meaningful one — because this was the first time I'd be on campus as a **student**, not a visitor, not someone checking the portal.

I was finally going to see the place I'd be spending the next 5 years.

## First Impression

**The school is neat.**

That was the first thing I noticed. Clean. Organized. Well-kept.

And then I noticed the **size**.

FUNAAB is **one of the largest universities in Nigeria by landmass**. I'd read that before, but seeing it is different. This place is huge. You can't just walk from one end to the other casually — you need to plan your movements.

For someone coming from a smaller environment, it was a lot to take in.

## Meeting My Classmates

While I was on campus, I met some of my **classmates**.

This was big for me — because up until now, they were just names and phone numbers in a WhatsApp group. Now they were real people. Faces. Voices. Handshakes.

I introduced myself to a few of them. Small conversations. Nothing deep. But it was a start.

These are the people I'll be spending the next 5 years with. The people I'll struggle through labs with, study with, and hopefully graduate with.

Meeting them — even briefly — made the whole thing feel more real.

## What I Came For

The trip had a purpose: **collect the urine bottle for my medical tests.**

I got it. Mission accomplished.

Then I returned home.

## What This Day Meant

It wasn't a dramatic day. No big moment. No ceremony. Just a quick trip, a small task, and a first glimpse.

But it mattered because:

- It was my **first time on campus as a student**
- It was my **first time meeting classmates in person**
- It was the **beginning of everything**

Sometimes the biggest moments aren't the loudest ones.

## What I Noticed

| Observation | Thought |
|-------------|---------|
| The campus is neat | FUNAAB takes care of its environment |
| The campus is massive | I'll need to learn the layout fast |
| My classmates are real people | Not just WhatsApp names anymore |
| The journey home | It hit me: this is actually happening |

## What's Next

- **Medical tests** — Monday, September 28
- **Accommodation** — sorting out the room at Oluwo
- **Lectures** — already started without me
- **Catching up** — the real work begins

## Final Thought

I didn't attend orientation. I missed the first week of lectures. I still don't have my own place.

But I stood on that campus for the first time as a student.

And that's something.
