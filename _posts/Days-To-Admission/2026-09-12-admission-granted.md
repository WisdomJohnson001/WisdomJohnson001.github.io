It's official. I've been admitted to study **Mechatronics Engineering** at the **Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB)**.

But the story of how I found out? That's worth documenting.

## The Waiting Game

For **one full month**, I checked two portals every single day:

- **JAMB portal** — constantly refreshing
- **FUNAAB portal** — constantly refreshing

Morning. Afternoon. Night. Sometimes multiple times a day.

If you've ever waited for admission in Nigeria, you know the feeling. The anxiety. The hope. The "maybe today" that turns into "maybe tomorrow."

## Being Proactive

Instead of just waiting and worrying, I decided to do something smart.

I started **searching for my department's WhatsApp group** — even before I was admitted.

Why? Because I wanted to be ahead. I wanted to know what was happening. I wanted to be part of the conversation, not just a spectator refreshing a portal.

**Lucky for me, I found one.**

I requested to join. I was accepted.

That decision changed everything.

## The Moment

One faithful day, I was scrolling through the group chat like I normally did — just staying updated, seeing what my future classmates were talking about.

Then I saw it.

My mates were already discussing their admissions.

My heart stopped.

I quickly switched to the FUNAAB portal. Checked. Refreshed.

**And boom.**

There it was.

**Admission offered.**

Mechatronics Engineering. FUNAAB. 2026/2027.

I sat there staring at the screen. One month of checking. One month of waiting. One month of "what if" — and it was finally there.

## Why This Matters

This taught me something important:

**Being proactive pays off.**

If I hadn't searched for that WhatsApp group, I might have waited days or weeks longer to check. I might have missed the moment. But because I was already in the room, I heard the news the moment it broke.

That's a lesson I'll carry through my engineering career:
- Don't just wait for things to happen
- Position yourself where information flows
- Be in the room before you need to be

## What's Next

- **Registration** — Paperwork, student ID, all of it
- **Orientation** — Learning the campus, meeting lecturers
- **First Lectures** — Actually sitting in a university classroom
- **Meeting Classmates** — The same people from that WhatsApp group, now in person

## My Commitment

I started this blog to document my journey. Now the journey is real.

From today, I'll write about:
- Every course, every lab, every project
- The wins and the failures
- What I'm learning and what I'm struggling with
- Resources for future FUNAAB students

This is Day 1 of the next 5 years.

Let's go. ⚙️🚀
